The Beginning

When the science club was about to open in April 2025, someone asked: "It's a science club, why not use technology? Why manual registration?" That moment sparked a determination to replace manual forms with a proper digital system and showcase our projects to the world.

Starting from the club's opening on April 15th, 2025, I embarked on this journey. 8 months of intense development alongside studies, followed by 1 month of polishing, and I'm still improving every day.
